```
Wazifly is an AI-powered career platform that connects Job Seekers and
Employers on one intelligent, bilingual app. Whether you're looking for your
next role or your next great hire, Wazifly puts smart, personalized tools
in your hands — in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.

── FOR JOB SEEKERS ──
Land the right role faster with a complete AI toolkit:

• Resume Analyzer — upload your CV and get an instant ATS score, strengths,
  gaps, missing skills, and clear improvement suggestions.
• AI Job Matching — see roles ranked to your profile with a match score and a
  plain-language reason for every match.
• Career Coach — chat with an AI mentor for guidance on skills, interviews,
  and career direction, personalized to your resume.
• CV Builder — generate a professional, ATS-friendly CV and export it as a PDF.
• Interview Prep — practice HR, technical, and behavioral questions with scored
  feedback and a personalized improvement plan.
• For You — a personalized hub of recommended jobs, skills to learn, courses,
  and a step-by-step career roadmap.
• Multiple CVs — keep several tailored resumes in one place and choose the right
  one for each application.
• Internships & Learning — browse internships and track the skills and topics
  you want to grow.

Browse and search jobs, save the ones you like, and track every application in
one place.

── FOR EMPLOYERS ──
Hire smarter with a full recruiting workspace:

• Company Profile — build a polished, verified-ready employer presence.
• Job Management — post, edit, publish, and manage your openings with
  auto-save and a live preview of exactly what candidates see.
• Applicants — review candidates per job with AI match insights, resume
  analysis, skills, and a clear status pipeline from review to offer.
• Analytics & AI Insights — track your hiring funnel, time-to-hire, and top
  jobs, with on-demand AI recruiter insights (strengths, bottlenecks, and
  suggested actions).

── BUILT FOR EVERYONE ──
• Fully bilingual: English and Arabic, with complete RTL layout.
• Clean, modern Material design that works offline for saved content.
• Secure sign-in — optional biometric (fingerprint/face) unlock protects your
  account.
• Privacy-conscious: analytics are consent-based, and your data is handled per
  our Privacy Policy.

── A NOTE ON AI ──
Wazifly uses AI to generate recommendations, analysis, and coaching to
assist your decisions. These are supportive suggestions to help you move
forward — they are not professional employment, career, or legal advice. Always
use your own judgment for important decisions.

Wazifly is free to use and ad-free.

Download Wazifly and build your future — one smart step at a time.
```
