## 1. Who this applies to

Wazifly is an AI-powered career platform for **Job Seekers** and
**Employers**. This Policy applies to both, and covers all features of the App.
The App is intended for users **18 years of age and older**; it is not directed
at children.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what the App needs to function. Depending on the features you use:

**a. Account & identity information**
- Email address (email sign-in), and/or phone number (phone sign-in), and/or your
  Google account basic profile (name, email, profile photo) if you use Google
  Sign-In.
- A unique user ID assigned by our authentication provider.

**b. Profile & career information (that you provide)**
- Display name, headline, location, bio, profile photo.
- Skills, experience level, preferred job titles, and portfolio/GitHub/LinkedIn
  links.
- **Résumé/CV content** you upload or create, and generated CVs.
- For employers: company profile (name, industry, size, website, description,
  contact, logo, social links) and the job postings you create.
- Applications, saved jobs, and related activity.

**c. Usage & diagnostics**
- App interaction/analytics events and screen views (**only when you consent** —
  see §6).
- Crash reports and performance/diagnostic data to keep the App stable.
- A push-notification token (if you enable notifications).

**d. What we do NOT collect**
- We do **not** collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, calendar,
  SMS/call logs, browsing history, health data, or financial/payment information.
- We do **not** use advertising identifiers, and the App contains **no ads**.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:
- Provide and operate the App's features (account, profiles, jobs, applications).
- **Power AI features** — résumé analysis, job matching, career coaching, CV
  building, interview preparation, recommendations, and (for employers) recruiter
  insights and analytics (see §5).
- Maintain security and integrity (e.g. app-instance attestation).
- Diagnose crashes and improve performance.
- Send notifications you have opted into.
- With your consent, understand aggregate usage to improve the App.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** share it with
third parties for their own independent marketing.

## 4. Service providers (data processors)

The App is built on **Google Firebase / Google Cloud** (project
`careerbridge-97-f58c9`), which processes data **on our behalf** as our backend:

- **Firebase Authentication** — sign-in and identity.
- **Cloud Firestore** — profiles, jobs, applications, company data.
- **Cloud Storage for Firebase** — résumés, profile photos, company logos.
- **Firebase Analytics** — consent-gated usage analytics.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — crash reporting.
- **Firebase Performance Monitoring** — diagnostics.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — push notifications.
- **Firebase App Check** — app-integrity attestation.
- **Firebase AI Logic (Google Gemini)** — the AI model that powers the App's
  intelligent features (see §5).

These providers process data under Google's terms and privacy commitments. See
Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 5. Artificial Intelligence features & transparency

Wazifly uses AI (Google's Gemini model, accessed via Firebase AI Logic) to
**generate recommendations, analysis, and coaching to assist you**. To do this,
content you provide — such as your **résumé text, profile details, and related
career information** — is transmitted to the AI service to produce a result (for
example, an ATS score, job matches, coaching replies, CV content, interview
feedback, or, for employers, recruiter insights).

**Important — AI is assistance, not professional advice.** AI-generated outputs
are **supportive suggestions to help you make your own decisions**. They may be
incomplete or inaccurate and **should not be considered professional employment,
career, hiring, or legal advice.** Always apply your own judgment, and consult a
qualified professional for important decisions. You are responsible for how you
use AI-generated content.

## 6. Your choices & consent

- **Analytics consent:** the App includes an analytics **consent lever**. If you
  decline, usage-analytics events are suppressed. You can change this choice in
  the App.
- **Notifications:** you control push notifications via your device settings and
  the App's notification preferences.
- **Profile data:** you choose what optional profile information to provide.

We use industry-standard safeguards. Data is **encrypted in transit** (TLS/HTTPS)
via Firebase, access is authenticated, and backend security rules restrict data
access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to
protect your information.
